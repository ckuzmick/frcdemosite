How to drive the TestBot

      
This robot runs the team's real drive, shooter and intake code. Pick up the yellow fuel, drive into the green ring around the blue hub, and shoot. Change any constant in the Tune panel on the right to feel what it does.

    


    
    
      
  1. Shoot right awayYou start with 8 fuel, already inside the green ring and facing the hub. Hold Space. The shooter spins up for 1 second, then feeds.
    2. 
      
  2. Collect more fuelThe intake is on the back of the robot, opposite the amber arrow. Press 2 to start the rollers, then back up (S) into the yellow balls in the middle of the field. They roll under the intake roller and get pulled in.
    3. 
      
  3. Score againDrive back into the green ring, face the hub, and hold Space. Or hold F and the camera will aim the robot for you.
